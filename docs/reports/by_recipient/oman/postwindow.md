## Post-window context (September 2026)

*The corpus extends to 2026-09-25; September is 25 days of data — context, not
analysis-grade. August 2026 is the last month inside the analysis window.*

- **The Muscat channel went quiet in September.** Iran→Oman ran 201 → 212 → 192 corroborated
  docs Jun→Aug, carried by the Iran–Oman Strait of Hormuz navigation talks (August's
  most-covered Iranian file in Oman), then fell to 14 in Sept 1–25 — a single Gulf/Oman Sea
  littoral-states foreign ministers' meeting. The Iran–US track did not stop; it moved:
  China's mediation of a return to the Islamabad Memorandum (Wang Yi–Araghchi, Tehran,
  material 8.0) and the UNGA week carried it in September. **Oman remains Iran's #3
  corroborated recipient in-window (1,701)**, behind Lebanon and Iraq, but its role as the
  default venue is now contested rather than settled.
