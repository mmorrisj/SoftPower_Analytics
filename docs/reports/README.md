## The single most important finding: raw volume lies

Ranked by **raw** media volume, Iran appears to dominate MENA influence. Ranked by
**third-party-corroborated** volume — coverage not originating from the actor's own state media —
the order inverts completely:

| Actor | Raw docs | Corroborated docs | Self-report share | Rank shift |
|-------|---------:|------------------:|:-----------------:|:----------:|
| **Turkey** | 43,098 | **36,518** | 0.15 | — (leads) |
| **China** | 30,279 | **26,005** | 0.14 | ▲ |
| **Russia** | 27,706 | **25,267** | 0.09 | ▲ |
| **Iran** | 83,017 | **13,854** | **0.83** | ▼▼▼ (last) |

Iran posts roughly double the raw volume of any rival but ranks **last** on corroborated
influence: 83% of its footprint is Iranian state media. (China holds the #2 slot it took in
the previous refresh, now ~740 docs ahead of Russia.) The self-report ratio is a genuine narrative-projection
signal **only for Iran** (82 Iran-geofocus outlets in the corpus) and weakly China (2 outlets);
Russia and Turkey have **zero** domestic-geofocus outlets ingested, so their low self-report
shares reflect corpus composition, not validation — for them, corroborated volume is simply the
intensity measure.

**Five influence models (the U.S. on a separate, incidental-capture basis):**
- **China — the economic patron.** Diplomacy/economics-led, ~0% military. Owns the **Gulf
  (Saudi, UAE) + Egypt + Jordan**. Volume is diplomatic; impact is economic megadeals (the $10B
  Egypt steel complex, SCO Tianjin $12.75B, $30.5B BRI contracts). Cedes the Levant.
- **Russia — the great-power broker.** Most diplomacy-heavy (62.6%), highly credible. Owns the
  **Iran relationship** (its #1 tie, 0.95 corroboration) and anchors **Egypt** via the El Dabaa
  nuclear plant. Narrow and deep.
- **Turkey — the conflict mediator.** Largest credible footprint overall. Dominates the
  **Levant's conflict files (Syria, Palestine, Iraq, Israel)** through humanitarian aid and
  mediation (Gaza talks at Sharm el-Sheikh, $216B Syria reconstruction). Coalition model
  (Turkey-Qatar-Egypt).
- **Iran — the religious projector.** Diplomacy/social-led; distinctive religious soft power
  (Arbaeen pilgrimage logistics, Red Crescent). Genuine traction only in the **Shia
  Resistance arc (Lebanon, Iraq)** — and even there, mostly self-reported.
- **United States — the crisis manager** *(incidental-capture basis; not directly comparable)*.
  ~78% diplomacy, near-zero economic patronage. Where it registers, it **out-covers every rival on
  the security/conflict files** (Iran 25,208; Israel 17,808; Palestine 12,321; Lebanon 9,451;
  Saudi 3,632) but **cedes the development files** — Egypt to China, Syria/Iraq to Turkey. Its
  image is 25% written by Iranian media, concentrated on the U.S.–Iran bilateral. The strategic
  pattern: **U.S. security + Chinese capital** is the Gulf's hedge. *(The mid-June 2026 US–Iran
  ceasefire MOU and July Lebanon–Israel framework mediation are the thesis in action.)*

## Strategic implications for U.S. policymakers
- **Discount Iran's apparent regional dominance** — it is largely a media-amplification effect;
  Iran's externally-validated influence is the weakest of the four and confined to its
  confessional core.
- **China's economic entrenchment in the Gulf and Egypt is the most credible, durable influence
  vector** and the least visible in conflict-driven reporting — watch the deals, not the headlines.
- **Turkey is the rising influence power in the Levant**, leveraging the Gaza and Syria files;
  its coalition with Qatar and rapprochement with Egypt are the dependencies to watch.
- **The Russia-Iran axis** is the densest single relationship in the dataset and increasingly
  structures both actors' regional behavior.

## Method & artifacts
- Charts plot corroborated metrics with raw shown for contrast; underlying numbers are persisted
  as sibling CSVs in each report's `assets/`.
- Derived analytics (provenance normalization, cross-actor tensor, entity graph, event-entity
  links) are documented with migration-ready DDL in
  [`_derived/manifest.md`](_derived/manifest.md); build scripts are reproducible in `_derived/`.
- **Caveats:** media reporting ≠ ground truth; the soft-power lens under-captures hard power
  (Iran proxies, Russia basing/arms, Turkey drones); monetary figures are *announced*, not
  verified (no in-window AidData overlap). Cross-actor magnitude is comparable only on the
  corroborated basis.
